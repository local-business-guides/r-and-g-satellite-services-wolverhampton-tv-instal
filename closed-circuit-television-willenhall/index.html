What a CCTV installation involves

A CCTV system can include cameras, connections, and a screen or recording device, depending on the design. The installer needs to understand which parts of a property matter to you and how the equipment can be fitted there. Key facts to discuss include:

  • What it covers: the cameras and associated equipment agreed for the areas you want monitored. The final arrangement depends on the site and system design.
  • Typical timing: scheduling and installation time vary with the number of devices, access, and how much cabling is needed. Ask for an estimate based on your property rather than relying on a general time allowance.
  • What affects cost: the number and type of cameras, recording or storage choices, cable routes, and installation access. Ask which items are included in the proposed work.
  • Terms you may hear: *field of view* means the area a camera can capture; a *DVR* or *NVR* is a type of recording device used with compatible systems; *cabling* connects system components where a wired setup is used.
