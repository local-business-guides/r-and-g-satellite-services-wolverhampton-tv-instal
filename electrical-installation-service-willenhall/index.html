What the work may cover and how it is planned

The work depends on the installation requested. Before arranging a visit, share any relevant details about the existing setup and the intended use. If the job needs an on-site look, the condition of the space and the route for any required cabling can help establish a suitable plan.

Key facts to discuss:
- What it covers: the specific equipment, connections, and installation tasks agreed for your job.
- Typical timing: the time needed depends on the size and complexity of the work, site access, and whether the existing setup needs changes. Confirm timing once the scope is understood.
- What affects cost: the amount of work, equipment and materials, access, cabling needs, and any changes found during planning. Ask for an estimate based on your particular requirements.

A few terms may come up. Scope means the tasks agreed for the job. Cabling refers to the cables needed to connect equipment. Existing setup means the equipment and connections already at the property. Ask for any unfamiliar term to be explained before you approve the work.

How long does an electrical installation take?
