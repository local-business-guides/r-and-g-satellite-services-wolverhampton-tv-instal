What the installation covers and how to plan it

A satellite installation may involve fitting or checking equipment and connecting it to the TV setup. The exact work depends on the service requested, existing equipment and the property. These are useful facts to establish before booking:

  • What it covers: satellite or Sky TV installation, with the details agreed for your home or commercial premises.
  • Typical timing: there is no single duration for every job. The work involved, existing equipment and access to the installation point can affect the appointment. Ask about timing when you describe the job.
  • What affects cost: the equipment needed, the amount of installation work, the condition of existing equipment and access requirements. Ask for the scope and any costs to be explained before work begins; no fixed price can be assumed from a general description.

When comparing providers, check that they understand the service you want, ask what is included, and find out how they handle existing equipment or access constraints. Compare like with like rather than relying only on a short description of the service.

A few terms may come up. A satellite dish is the outdoor receiving equipment. A receiver is equipment used to pass the signal to a television. Sky TV refers to the TV service named in the installation request, so be clear about the setup you currently have or want.
